Spark - AnagramPlug Test

Anagramplug is a word-based puzzle that challenges players to plug the correct anagram into
a given word fragment or prefix/suffix to form a complete, meaningful word.
How it works: A partial word is presented (e.g., Inti___), and multiple options are given.
Each option is an anagram — a rearrangement of letters — and only one of them,
when “plugged” into the blank, completes a real word.

Example:

Inti___
A. meat
B. mate
C. team

Correct Answer: B. mate → Intimate

Anagramplug = Plugging the right anagram into the gap to complete a meaningful word. It sharpens spelling, pattern recognition, and vocabulary skills in a creative way.
