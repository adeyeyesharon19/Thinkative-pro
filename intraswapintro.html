Flame - IntraSwap Test

IntraSwap Branding is a strategic brand-naming technique where a single letter in a
word is replaced with another letter already present within the same word.
This subtle internal shift transforms a familiar term into a relevant,
industry-aligned brand name while preserving its original structure,
rhythm, and memorability.

Example:

1GIGAB(Y)TE–( For a fast food company,)

swap which letter to get a tasty brand?

Swap Y for: A. T

B. B

C. U

D. I ✅

Correct Answer: D. I Brand Name created:GIGABITE

GIGAB(Y)TE → GIGABITE

The replacement letter (I) already exists inside Gigabyte, creating a natural,
intuitive brand evolution rather than a forced invention.
