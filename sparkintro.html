Spark - Back To Front Test

Back To Front Test is a creative wordplay activity where a word, when spelled in reverse,
forms another valid word. It helps sharpen attention to detail, spelling,
and pattern recognition.

Example:

POT spelled backward becomes TOP.

Both are real words, and reversing one gives you the other — that’s the essence of the Back To Front Test.

It’s a fun and engaging way to explore language through reverse thinking.
