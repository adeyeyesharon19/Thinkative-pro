Flame - WordpickIQ Test

WordPick IQ is a vocabulary-based word game where learners are presented with a mix of real and
unreal anagram-like words, and must identify the correct, valid word among them.

Example:
Which of the following is a valid word?
- A. Stopage
- B. Topsage
- C. Optsage
- D. Postage ✅
Answer: D. Postage
WordPickIQ aims at Strengthens vocabulary recognition, Enhances spelling accuracy , Develops critical filtering skills,
Improves decision-making under word constraintsive Flexibility E.T.C.
"WordPick IQ — Spot the real, reject the fake, sharpen your word sense!"
