Spark - Before & Brand Test

Before & Brand is a creative brand-naming technique where a single letter in an existing
word is replaced with the letter that comes before it in the alphabet (or a strategically
chosen earlier letter) to form a new, meaningful brand name that aligns with a specific
industry or business purpose.

This method transforms familiar words into distinct, memorable brand identities while preserving pronunciation, recognition, and relevance.

Example:

Ap(O)state – For the App Development Industry

Replace O with:

A. E

B. P ✅

C. S

D. T

Correct Answer: B. (p)

Brand Name Created: APPSTATE

Why Before & Brand Works

Maintains familiarity while adding originality. Creates industry-relevant brand names.
Easy to remember and pronounce. Encourages creative thinking and wordplay
